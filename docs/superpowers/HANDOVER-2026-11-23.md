## The one that must be fixed before anything ships

**The Fukyaa promo does not actually expire.**

`src/app/fukyaa/page.tsx` and `src/lib/fukyaa.ts` call `new Date()` during
render, and `/fukyaa` is statically prerendered — the reviewer confirmed
`"compute": "static"` in `.next/prerender-manifest.json` and found 500 USD
already frozen into `.next/server/app/fukyaa.html`.

So on 1 January 2027 the page keeps quoting 500 USD and keeps saying the
promotion runs "until 2026-12-31", a date in the past, until someone
redeploys. That is the exact failure the module's own comment describes, and
it defeats the guarantee given to the owner that the figure would fall back
on its own.

The unit test passes because it calls `fukyaaDeposit(date)` directly and never
touches the rendering path. The lesson is bigger than the bug: a pure-function
test cannot see a build-time freeze.

Fix: `export const revalidate = 3600` on the page, plus a test that asserts
the route is not an immutable static prerender. Or render both figures
unconditionally with the cutover in prose, which needs no clock.

## Also found, not yet fixed

1. `products.ts` hardcodes "Minimum 500 USD deposit" for Fukyaa, which
   contradicts `commercial.ts` once the promo lapses (standard is 1000). That
   string renders on `/coaching`. Make `commercial.ts` the single source and
   derive `requirement` from it.
2. The cent column on the homepage never states the 500 USD minimum — the one
   qualifier that decides whether a visitor should bother.
3. The "two accounts" warning sits *below* both columns and their CTAs, so a
   visitor who scans and clicks has already acted. Move it above the grid.
4. `ProductGuide` says "The steps below change with it" but renders both
   routes unconditionally, and the route cards are plain divs, not links.
5. `ProductGuide` owns the closing CTA, so each page's own sections render
   *after* the ask — including Copytrade's fee terms, the most important
   commercial content on the site.
6. The Copytrade worked example reads as an earnings illustration: flat
   assertions rather than conditionals, and the "stays with you" row is
   bolded like a returns takeaway.
7. The Copytrade copy implies an answer to the high-water-mark question
   nobody answered — "if the week does not gain, there is nothing to share"
   tells a reader a loss carries nothing forward. Get the real answer or
   soften it.
8. `fukyaa.promoEndsOn` renders raw as "2026-12-31" instead of a readable
   date, and its boundary is UTC so the promo lingers until 08:00 local on
   1 January.
9. `product-band.tsx` is dead code; `AccountGateway` replaced it.
