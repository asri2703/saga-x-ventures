## 24 November

**The whole homepage was restructured, section by section, to the owner's
own copy.** Each change below is one commit; the commit message carries the
reasoning.

- **The hero** gained the Uiverse graph-paper background, moved its two float
  cards below the portrait with the product marks in their discs, and had its
  paragraph rewritten and then polished. "We believe" is now "I believe".
- **A logo strip** under the hero: seven marks, two rows, translated by half
  its width so the join is invisible.
- **"What I build"** is now a three-card showcase built from four real
  screens exported from the live products, not drawn mock-ups. The cards are
  cut off at the bottom behind a fade, deliberately: a product screen shrunk
  until it all fits is one nobody can read.
- **"Who I am"** takes his three roles — economic trader, educator, fund
  manager — as a stacked list on the cream panel.
- **"Get started"** is now the packages, which is the biggest change on the
  page. See below.
- **"Three conversations" is deleted.** He asked for its content to go, and
  with the three panels gone there was no section left to keep. One line went
  with it that was not placeholder copy and is worth putting back somewhere:
  *"What I will not do is tell you what you are going to earn. I do not know
  what I am going to earn either."* Recoverable from commit `e162a08`.
- **`account-gateway.tsx` and the account fork are deleted**, replaced by the
  packages. The two-accounts warning survives as the paragraph above them,
  and the ordering test that held it before the columns now holds it before
  the packages.
- **Motion moved to GSAP** with Lenis for smooth scroll. See the README's
  Motion section before touching it; there is one trap about content that
  mounts late.
- **The whole site moved to glass**, because the owner said the cards sank
  into the background. One cause was literal: `Section tone="raised"`
  rendered the same colour `.panel` filled itself with, so on a banded
  section every card was exactly the colour of the band behind it. The band
  now sits a step *down* from the paper. The glass itself is built for pale
  paper rather than copied from a kit built for photographs — a 0.72 fill, a
  lit top edge on every pane, and a near-invisible wash on `body::before` so
  the blur has something to work on. Solid fallbacks for
  `@supports not (backdrop-filter)` and `prefers-reduced-transparency`.
- **`hover:bg-sage` on three `.panel` links had never worked.** `.panel` sets
  its background outside Tailwind's layers, so it beat the utility whatever
  order the classes were written in. Now `.panel-link` in `globals.css`, with
  `.panel-active` and `.panel-raised` beside it for the same reason. This is
  the third time this equal-specificity trap has cost something; if a
  Tailwind utility seems to be doing nothing on an element that also has a
  `globals.css` class, this is why.
- **A `Section` padding bug** was found and fixed architecturally. Sixteen
  sections across the site were rendering at default spacing while their
  source read as though they had been tightened, because `py-16` beats
  `py-10` in the compiled stylesheet whatever order the classes are written
  in. Padding now goes through a `pad` prop, held by
  `section-padding.test.ts`. This is the second time the same equal-
  specificity defect has cost a session; the first was a pine-on-pine ghost
  button.

### The packages

Mastery Signal sells three terms — RM99 a month, RM492 for six months, RM876
for twelve — with the per-month equivalent under each as a comparison. Fukyaa
has three ways in that differ by running cost rather than by deposit alone:
500 USD with a rented server, 1,000 USD with the server included
(recommended), or Copytrade at 500 USD with 30% of the gain settled weekly.
A switch chooses which product's packages are showing.

Twelve months is the marked recommendation and renders in the middle of the
row, lifted, at the owner's instruction. Where a plan is declared and where it
sits are separate: `commercial.ts` declares shortest term to longest because
that is what the arithmetic reads, and `centreRecommended()` moves the marked
one to the middle at render.

Every figure is in `commercial.ts`; `plans.ts` builds the copy from those
figures. The savings are derived from the monthly rate rather than stored,
and a test holds them to the arithmetic.

## The review has been actioned

All ten findings are fixed, on 23 November 2026. 91 tests pass, build green,
lint clean.

**The Fukyaa promo now expires.** `/`, `/fukyaa` and `/coaching` export
`revalidate = 3600`, which the build confirms (`Revalidate 1h` on all three,
`initialRevalidateSeconds: 3600` in the prerender manifest). The guard is
`src/app/__tests__/freshness.test.ts`: it walks each page's import graph, and
any page that can reach a `new Date()` or `Date.now()` must export a
`revalidate` literal of at most a day. It catches indirect reads, which is
what the original bug was — `fukyaaRoutes()` defaults its `now` argument.

The rest, in the order they were listed:

1. `Product.requirement` is now `(now: Date) => string | null`, derived from
   `commercial.ts`. A test fails on any deposit figure in `products.ts` that
   is not one of the three declared in the terms.
2. The fork renders each product's requirement, so the cent column states
   its minimum. *(The fork is gone as of the 24th.)*
3. The two-accounts warning moved above the grid. Held by an ordering test.
4. The route cards are links to their own step sections, and the copy no
   longer claims the steps swap.
5. `ProductGuide` takes an `afterIntro` slot. Copytrade's fee terms, Fukyaa's
   costs and Mastery Signal's features all render before the closing ask
   instead of after it.
6. The Copytrade example is conditional throughout ("If an account were
   funded with...") and nothing in it is bolded.
7. The high-water-mark question is named as the broker's rule and offered in
   writing, rather than answered. **This still needs the real answer.**
8. The deadline renders as "31 December 2026" and expires at midnight in
   Malaysia rather than eight the next morning.
9. `product-band.tsx` deleted.

Two pre-existing lint errors in `overlapping-slider.tsx` went with them:
`performance.now()` in a component body is now `e.timeStamp`, which the event
already carries.
